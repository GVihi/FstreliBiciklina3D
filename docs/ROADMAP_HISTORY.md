## Milestone K: Procedural Arenas › Gameplay on open ground

### Rider pursuit steering: ride toward the player on any open surface, pass, then turn round in a smooth arc and return; spawning at gates

`Arena.rider_pursuit()` switches it on, so an arena that says yes gets it and the roundabout, which says no, keeps its lanes; the code is in `scripts/riders.gd`. A rider comes out of one of the arena's gates (drawn with the seed) facing in, rides at the player, and once the player is behind it and within 7 m it rides straight on for 9 m, then swings round and comes back, pass after pass. The turn is limited by sideways acceleration (`PURSUIT_ACCEL` 20 m/s², at most 6 rad/s), so a fast rider swings wide (a turning circle of speed squared over 20 m) and a slow one turns tight, never a snap.
